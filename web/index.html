DIY MODE

Custom Per-Key RGB Lighting

Record your own custom color scheme directly to the keyboard's flash memory without any software needed on Windows.

  1. 1
    Enter Recording Mode

    Press Fn2 + \. The keyboard LEDs will start blinking to indicate recording is ready.

  2. 2
    Set Per-Key Colors

    Tap any key repeatedly to cycle through its available LED colors until you get the color you want.

  3. 3
    Save & Exit

    Press Fn2 + \ once more. Your custom layout is saved directly to the K530 onboard memory!

💡 Tip: You can turn the backlight completely off anytime with Fn2 + 0 to maximize Bluetooth battery life.

📶

Bluetooth Pairing & Switching

Switch to Bluetooth: Set side toggle switch to "B"
Pair Profile 1 (BT1): Hold Fn + Q for 3s (LED flashes fast)
Pair Profile 2 (BT2): Hold Fn + W for 3s
Pair Profile 3 (BT3): Hold Fn + E for 3s
Switch Between Devices: Short tap Fn + Q / W / E
🔋

Battery Check Gauge

Because third-party keyboards don't always broadcast battery via macOS Bluetooth menus, the K530 has a built-in hardware battery indicator:

Press: Fn2 + Esc
1: 10% 2: 20% 3: 30% 4: 40% 5: 50% 6: 60% 7: 70% 8: 80% 9: 90% 0: 100%

Number keys 1 through 0 will illuminate in green/red showing the exact charge remaining.
